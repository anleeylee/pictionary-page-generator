How to play Space Pictionary

  1. Split into two teams. Each team picks a drawer and a scorekeeper; the drawer changes every round.
  2. Draw a card or spin the generator. The drawer silently reads the prompt from the list below (or the generator) and must not say the word, spell it, or make sounds.
  3. Sketch, don't write. Draw pictures only — letters and numbers are not allowed, and the clock starts as soon as the prompt is revealed.
  4. Guess out loud. Teammates call out guesses; the drawer may nod or shake their head but can never speak.
  5. Score a point when the word is guessed within the time limit (60–90 seconds works well), then pass the turn.

First team to the agreed target (usually 10 points) wins. For rules, house variants and printable score sheets see how to play Pictionary.

Space Pictionary FAQ

What level are the Space Pictionary words?

The library mixes easy, medium and hard prompts so every round has a fair spread: simple concrete nouns for warm-ups, trickier actions and abstract ideas for the final rounds. You can also open the Pictionary Word Generator to draw a random word at any difficulty.

What space words are easy to draw?

Stick to single concrete nouns with strong shapes: Moon, rocket, star and Saturn are almost instantly recognizable on paper. Save nebulae and black holes for the hard rounds.

How do you draw an abstract space word like 'gravity'?

Draw an apple falling past a planet, or a ball rolling off a table — players usually get it fast once they see the motion cue.
